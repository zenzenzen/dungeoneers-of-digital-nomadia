# Domain

**Source asset:** a traceable original CSV row, prose section, template entry, or visual. Variants and duplicate representations remain linked.

**Card definition:** approved party content with separate flavor, executable effects, source references, quantities, and art inputs. Classes, traits, equipment, monsters, events, actions, and finales have separate pools.

**Campaign:** pools, difficulty thresholds, economy, timers, goals, and endings. **Run:** one shuffled game; power and wallets reset on rematch. **Collection:** browser-local discovered cards and endings, never competitive strength.

**Chill:** encounter resilience; temporary Chill absorbs losses first. **Triggered:** zero Chill causes loss and a random action discard, then recovery on the player's next personal turn. **Help deal:** one consenting helper, with server-settled victory payment. **Intervention:** one action or paid protection per participant, resolved in server order. **Finale:** level-gated encounter awarding one romance winner.

The party rules live in `rules/party_game.md`. Legacy source uses D&D terminology that must not leak into executable party mechanics.
