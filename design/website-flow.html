Seats into Session Room (Lane D)
Design intent: every detail page is a hub, not a dead end — monsters link to the campaigns they appear in, items link to the rules they trigger, campaigns deploy straight into the Session Room. The satire stays on the surface (fake traction metrics, "Operational Risk" panels, waitlist-for-enlightenment copy) while the data stays canonical underneath. Primary actions are always WiFi Blue; game-state moments glow Mango; danger reads Coral.
