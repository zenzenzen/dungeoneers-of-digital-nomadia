# Human playtest sheet

Status: not yet performed. Automated browser sessions are not human playtests.

Run one three-player session and one six-player session with fresh players. Use ordinary turn timers and the shipped card pool. Do not coach strategy; explain controls only when someone is stuck.

Record per session:

- Date, content version, player count, devices, and whether people shared a voice call.
- Time from lobby start to ending; target is 25–40 minutes.
- Time to first understood encounter; slowest turn and reason.
- Number of completed rounds; winner or unsuccessful ending.
- Whether each player understood class/trait bonuses, help payments, and the intervention window.
- Repeated losses, long waits, confusing card wording, and moments that required a human rules ruling.
- Funniest preserved joke; weakest new joke; most memorable betrayal.
- Whether players would immediately rematch, and why.

Acceptance: both party sizes can finish without a DM or developer intervention. Most of the group should understand legal actions after their first turn. Record observed duration honestly; adjust campaign numbers only after identifying the source of delay. If long waits dominate, shorten decisions or simplify the choice before adding more content.

Use a short issue list: observation, evidence, proposed adjustment, and the next test that would establish improvement. Keep suggested changes separate from shipped rules.
